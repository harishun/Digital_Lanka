Roll based access

Administrator
•Manages all accounts.
•Manage the systems and data base.
•Override any activity, subject to an immutable audit log that permanently records all administrative actions to prevent abuse.

Law enforcement officers
•Provisioned by centralized IT using their designation and batch numbers (replaces self-registration to prevent impersonation).
•View a user's Documents; access must expire after a pre-determined time to prevent misconduct.
•Access an offline-fallback mode (e.g., license plate lookup or offline QR scan) for use in cellular dead zones or if the user's device is dead.
•Issue penalties for traffic violations and view previous violations.
•Update the status of a pending violation.
•The issued violation should appear on the officer's dashboard.
•The officer should extend the general user.

General User
•Register using the NIC; the system automatically verifies identity by connecting to the Mock Government Database API.
•The system automatically links vehicle ownership, driving license, revenue license, and insurance directly from the Mock API (replaces manual photo uploads to prevent document fraud).
•Grant or revoke access to other users to drive their owned registered vehicles, including the ability to issue frictionless temporary passes for valets, mechanics, or emergencies.
•Any unauthorized users driving the vehicle should be reported to the system and notified to the owner.
•If the vehicle is reported stolen or missing, the user could be detained (requires a strict, verified protocol to clear the stolen status if the owner recovers the vehicle themselves).

The Penalty

Include the date and time of the Violation.
Include the place of the violation.
Include the Nature of the Violation.
Include the name & batch number of the Issuing Officer.
Provide the Reference number for payments.
Automatically update payment status from PENDING to PAID via Webhook integration from the GovPay Mock Service (replaces manual review of submitted payment proof).

Future Improvements

Pay the violation fines through the app.
